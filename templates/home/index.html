{# ===================== FAQ ===================== #}

FAQ

Questions buyers ask first.

Still stuck? Send us a message.

What is a VIN?

A VIN is the 17-character vehicle identification number. It is unique to the car and lets us look up its record.

Do I need an account to check a vehicle?

No. You can check a vehicle and buy any pack without logging in. Your email keeps your credits and reports accessible for later.

How do credits work?

One credit unlocks one report. Credits are added to your account after payment and are valid for 4 months.

What if I cannot find the VIN?

Use the license plate tab and select the state. We will look up the vehicle from the plate and state.
