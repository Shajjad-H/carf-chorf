2 What you buy

The vehicle lookup is free. It shows basic vehicle information and the number of records available for the vehicle. Check this information before you buy.

You can then buy prepaid credits, alone or in packs, at the prices shown on the pricing page. One credit unlocks one vehicle history report for one vehicle.

A report contains the records available from our third-party data providers at the time it is generated. Depending on the vehicle, it may include accidents and damage, mileage readings, ownership, title information, and service records. Not every section is available for every vehicle.

3 Prices

Prices are shown in US dollars (USD) and confirmed in the order summary before payment. Any applicable sales tax is shown in the order summary before payment. The price shown when you place your order is the price you pay. We may change prices at any time for future orders.

We charge no additional fees.

4 How an order works

  1. You check a vehicle by VIN, or by license plate and state.
  2. You choose a credit pack.
  3. You enter your email address, or you are signed in.
  4. You review the order summary and total, and can correct your choices.
  5. You tick the box accepting these Terms and Terms of Sale.
  6. You pay through our third-party payment service.

The contract is concluded when your payment is confirmed. We send you a confirmation by email.

We may refuse or cancel an order in case of suspected fraud, a payment problem or a legal restriction. If we cancel an order before delivery, we refund it in full.

5 Payment

Payment is made in full when you order through our third-party payment service. We do not receive or store your full card details.

If a payment fails, is cancelled or is reversed, the order is not delivered, or access to it is suspended.

6 Delivery and confirmation

Delivery is digital and immediate once payment is confirmed: your credits are added to your account and, when your order is linked to a vehicle, its report is generated straight away. You can open your reports from your account and from the link we email you.

We send you an order confirmation email with the link to access your report later. Your reports remain available in your account for as long as your account is active. If we need to delete them, for example if we stop the Service or close an inactive account, we will email you at least 30 days in advance so you can download them. This does not apply to accounts closed under the "Acceptable use and suspension" section of our Terms of Service.

Keep your report link private: anyone with it may be able to view the report. If you do not receive your confirmation within 24 hours, check your spam folder, then contact us.

7 No right of withdrawalImportant

Reports and credits are digital content delivered immediately. By placing an order, you request immediate delivery. Orders cannot be cancelled once paid, except as provided in sections 8 and 9 and in our Terms of Service.

8 After a report is generated: contact usImportant

Once a report has been generated, the sale is final in principle and is not automatically refundable.

If you changed your mind, bought the wrong pack, mistyped the VIN, license plate or state, or have a concern about the report content, contact us within 30 days with your order details. We review these requests case by case and may, at our discretion, offer a courtesy solution such as a credit, a corrected or re-run report, or a refund. This is not an automatic right and does not guarantee a refund.

Contact us before opening a payment dispute.

9 When the error is ours

We make it right when:

  • a technical failure on our side prevents a report from being generated or delivered
  • we deliver a report for a different vehicle from the one you entered
  • you were charged twice for the same order.

Depending on the case, we deliver or re-run the report, restore the credit, or refund the amount concerned within 14 days, using your original payment method. Contact us within 30 days with your order email, the VIN or plate, and a description of the problem.

This does not affect any rights you have under mandatory consumer law.

10 Credits

  • Credits are prepaid units added to your account once payment is confirmed. One credit unlocks one report.
  • A credit is used when a report is generated, even if you never open the report.
  • Credits are valid for 4 months from the date of purchase. Credits not used within that period expire automatically, with no refund or extension. Any reminder email we send is a courtesy, not receiving it does not extend validity.
  • Credits are personal and non-transferable. They have no cash value and cannot be resold, exchanged or refunded, except as provided in sections 8 and 9.
  • Your balance is shown in your account.

11 Your responsibilities

  • Check the VIN, license plate and state before paying, as well as the vehicle information and record count shown by the free lookup.
  • Use an email address you control and keep access to it.
  • Keep your report links private and use reports only as allowed by our Terms of Service.

12 What a report is, and is not

A report is a snapshot of the records available from our data providers when it is generated, it is not updated afterwards. It is an information tool, not an inspection, appraisal or warranty. Our Terms of Service (section “Vehicle data and no warranty”) apply in full.

We may suspend your account while the dispute is open and recover the amounts and costs owed, as set out in our Terms of Service.
