1 Who is responsible

The data controller is [[COMPANY_NAME]], [[REGISTERED_ADDRESS]] (see our Legal Notice). For any privacy question or request, write to [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]] or use our contact page.

2 What we collect

  • Account and order data: email address, one-time verification codes (stored in hashed form), orders, credits, reports generated.
  • Vehicle lookups: VIN, or license plate and state, and the vehicle information returned. This information concerns vehicles, we do not look up or provide owner names.
  • Payment data: transaction references and amounts provided by our third-party payment service. We never receive your full card details.
  • Support data: the email address, phone number and message you send through the contact form.
  • Technical and security data: IP address, browser information, logs, and the signals used by Cloudflare Turnstile to block bots.
  • On your device only: the last VINs you looked up, saved in your browser’s local storage so you can reuse them. They are not sent to us, and you can clear them at any time.

4 Who receives your data

We share data only with service providers that process it on our behalf (hosting, payment, email delivery, security and vehicle data). Vehicle data providers receive only the VIN or license plate and state. We do not sell your data.

5 Transfers outside the EU

Your data is stored in the United States. These transfers are protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Contact us for more information.

7 Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to give instructions about what happens to your data after your death. Write to [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]], we may ask you to prove your identity. We reply within one month.

Deleting your account also deletes access to your reports and unused credits. Data we must keep by law, such as invoices, is archived for the required period.

You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

8 Cookies and local storage

We only use what the Service needs to work: a session cookie to keep you signed in, a security cookie protecting forms (CSRF), the elements needed by Cloudflare Turnstile, and your recent VINs in local storage. These are strictly necessary, so they do not require your consent.

9 Security

We protect your data with appropriate measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed verification codes, access restricted to authorized personnel, and bot protection. No system is completely secure, if a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the authorities as required by law.

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. We will inform you of any significant change.
